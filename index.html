Privacy Policy

PSF Scan mobile app — Parivar Suraksha Foundation

Effective date: 24 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the PSF Scan Android app (the "App") handles, why, how it is protected, and what choices you have. The App is published by Parivar Suraksha Foundation ("PSF", "we", "us"), a not-for-profit company based in Surat, Gujarat, India. By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

PSF Scan is a tool for authorised PSF volunteers and staff. It scans a member's QR code and shows that member's details from the PSF member system. The App has no registration, no account and no login, so it does not ask for or store any user name or password.

2. Information we handle

The App collects very little. Most of what you see on screen is member information that already exists in the PSF member system and is only displayed to an authorised user.

a) Information you provide through the App

  • Text inside a scanned QR code. When you scan a member's QR code, the text it contains is sent to the PSF server so the matching member can be found.

b) Member details shown after a scan

Depending on what PSF holds for that member and what you are authorised to see, the App may display the member's name, member number and status, contact and address details, profile photo, identity and other uploaded documents, nominee details and health declaration. This information is loaded from PSF's servers and shown on screen. The App never edits it. Passwords, PINs, OTPs and access tokens are removed from server replies and are never shown or saved.

c) Information stored on your device

  • Scan History — a list of your recent scans (up to the latest 500), kept so you can look back at them.
  • Language choice — English, Hindi or Gujarati.
  • Temporary image cache — member photos and documents you open may be cached briefly on the device so they load faster.

This data stays in the App's private storage, is excluded from Android cloud backup, and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. You can also delete Scan History inside the App.

d) Technical information

Like any internet service, PSF's servers receive your IP address and basic request details (such as time and the request made) when the App contacts them. This is used to run and protect the service.

e) What we do not collect

The App does not collect your location, contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone recordings, calendar, advertising ID, or any photos from your gallery. The camera preview is used only to read QR codes — no photo or video is taken or stored. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

3. How we use information

  • To look up and show the member whose QR code you scan.
  • To show your own Scan History and remember your language choice.
  • To keep the service secure, working properly and free from misuse.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Information is not used for advertising and is never sold.

4. App permissions

PermissionWhy the App needs it

The App does not need storage, location, contacts, microphone or notification permissions.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with advertisers. Information is shared only:

  • with the technology and hosting providers that run PSF's servers, under confidentiality and security obligations;
  • with government, regulatory or law-enforcement authorities when the law requires it; and
  • with anyone else only with the consent of the person concerned.

The scanned QR text is sent only to PSF's own server, and member photos and documents are loaded only from PSF's own servers.

6. How we protect information

  • All communication between the App and PSF's servers is encrypted using HTTPS.
  • Data kept on the device is in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is excluded from Android cloud backup.
  • Access to member records is limited to authorised persons of the Foundation and its service providers.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Member details are personal and sensitive, so please use the App only for authorised purposes, keep your phone protected with a screen lock, and never share or photograph member information outside of your duties.

7. How long we keep information

On your device, Scan History is kept until you delete it, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Member records are held in PSF's member system for as long as the person is a member and afterwards as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires (for example accounting and regulatory records).

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Delete your local data — clear Scan History in the App, or clear the App's data / uninstall it from your phone settings.
  • Control permissions — turn the camera permission on or off any time in your phone's settings.
  • Ask about member records — a member can ask to see, correct or delete the details PSF holds about them, withdraw consent, or raise a complaint or grievance.

To make such a request, email psk4mail@gmail.com from the email address or phone number registered with PSF. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Because the App has no accounts, there is no in-app account to delete.

9. Children

The App is meant for adult PSF volunteers and staff (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children through the App.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address with the "Last updated" date changed. If we later add features that change how information is handled (for example a sign-in), this policy will be updated before those features are released.
